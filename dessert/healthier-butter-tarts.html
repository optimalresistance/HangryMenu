Healthier Butter Tarts (With Homemade Tart Shells) ▾

Ingredients
Homemade Tart Shells (Makes 6 small tarts)
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 cup cold butter, cubed
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2–3 tbsp cold water (add gradually)
Tart Filling
  • 1 egg
  • 1/3 cup maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp melted butter
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/4 cup raisins or chopped dates
  • 2 tbsp chopped walnuts (optional)
Recipe
  1. Make the Pastry Shells:
    1. Mix flour and salt in a bowl.
    2. Add cold butter and rub with fingertips until crumbly.
    3. Add cold water 1 tbsp at a time until it forms a dough.
    4. Wrap and chill 10 minutes.
    5. Roll out thinly and cut circles to fit your tart tin.
    6. Press the dough into 6 tart molds.
    7. Prick the bases with a fork.
    8. Bake *blind* at 190°C / 375°F for 8 minutes (optional but keeps bottoms crisp).
  2. Make the Filling:
    1. Whisk egg, maple syrup, brown sugar, melted butter, vanilla, and salt.
    2. Stir in raisins/dates and walnuts.
  3. Assemble:
    1. Pour filling into tart shells (2/3 full).
  4. Bake:
    1. Bake at 190°C / 375°F for 15–18 minutes until golden.
    2. Cool before serving.
Dessert Serves 3
