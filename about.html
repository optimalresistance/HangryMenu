This website provides five weeks of dinner ideas, each scaled for three people. Every week includes two dessert recipes and a complete shopping list for all meals. I built it to help plan weekly menus, simplify grocery trips, and have something reliable to fall back on when I’m not feeling creative. Although the menu is only for my wife and I, we cook for approximately 3-4 people so we can have leftovers for next day's lunch or dinner. This way, we only have to cook 4 times a week instead of 7.Breakfast is usually some variation of eggs, toast, bagels with cream cheese, and potoatoes. Or, it's a traditional Syrian breakfast with 'hawadir': olives, feta cheese, magdoos, etc. Lastly, salad is a given with dinner meals. We keep it

simply by chopping salad, cucumbers, tomatos, (feta and crutons sometimes), with a drizzle of olive oil, lemon, and pinch of salt and pepper.
