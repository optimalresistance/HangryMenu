Week 1 — Shopping List ▾

Proteins
  • Ground beef – ~500 g (meatballs + shish barak)
  • Beef strips – 300 g (stir-fry)
  • Chicken breast/thigh – 400 g (butter chicken)
  • 1 egg (meatballs)
  • 2 eggs (desserts)
Dairy
  • Parmesan – small block or 1/4 cup grated
  • Butter – at least 1/2 cup total (cookies + tarts + cooking)
  • Plain yogurt – 2.5 to 3 cups (shish barak)
  • Cream or coconut milk – 1/2 cup (butter chicken)
Grains / Pasta / Baking
  • Spaghetti – 200 g
  • Basmati rice – 1 cup
  • Flour – at least 4 cups (shish barak dough + cookies + tart shells)
  • Breadcrumbs – 1/4 cup
  • Rolled oats – 1.5 cups
  • Baking powder – 1/2 tsp
  • Baking soda – 1/4 tsp
  • Tart shells – 6 small (store-bought OR ingredients to make)
Vegetables & Herbs
  • Onions – 5 to 6 large (meatballs, butter chicken, shish barak)
  • Garlic – 1 whole bulb
  • Fresh ginger – small knob
  • Fresh coriander (cilantro) – 1 bunch (shish barak + stir-fry)
  • Mixed stir-fry vegetables – 2 cups
Pantry / Canned Goods
  • Crushed tomatoes – 1 can (400 mL)
  • Tomato paste – 1 small can/tube
  • Maple syrup – at least 3/4 cup (cookies + tarts)
  • Brown sugar – 1/4 cup
  • Walnuts – ~3/4 cup chopped (cookies + tarts)
  • Raisins or chopped dates – 1/4 cup (tarts)
  • Soy sauce – small bottle
  • Oyster sauce – small bottle
  • Olive oil
  • Cornstarch – at least 2 tablespoons (shish barak + stir-fry)
Spices & Seasonings
  • Salt & pepper
  • Basil, oregano (meatballs sauce)
  • Garam masala, cumin, chili powder (butter chicken)
  • Allspice or cinnamon (shish barak filling)
  • Dried mint (optional for yogurt)
  • Garlic powder (optional)
