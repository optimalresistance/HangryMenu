Shish Barak — Homemade Dough, Meat Filling & Yogurt Sauce ▾

Ingredients
For the Dough
  • 1 cup flour
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Pinch of salt
  • Warm water (as needed to form dough)
For the Meat Stuffing
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • Ground lamb or beef (about 250 g)
  • Fresh coriander, chopped
  • Salt & spices (allspice or cinnamon)
  • Oil for frying
For Shaping the Dumplings
  • Roll dough very thin
  • Cut 1.5-inch circles using a small tea cup
  • Remove excess dough around circles
  • Lift circles to prevent sticking
  • Place 1 tsp meat in center
  • Fold over, seal edges well, then pinch ends together
  • Bake lightly until pale golden
For the Yogurt Sauce
  • 2 cups yogurt
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • Pinch of salt
Final Flavoring
  • 3 cloves garlic, crushed
  • Fresh coriander (cilantro)
Recipe
  1. Make the Dough: Mix flour, oil, and salt. Add warm water gradually until dough forms. Knead briefly and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Prepare Stuffing: Fry onions in oil until soft. Add ground beef/lamb, chopped coriander, spices, and salt. Cook until browned.
  3. Shape Dumplings: Roll dough thin. Cut 1.5-inch circles with a tea cup. Place meat in center, fold, seal edges, and pinch ends together.
  4. Dry the Dumplings: Bake briefly until slightly golden (this prevents them from breaking in yogurt).
  5. Make Yogurt Base: In a pot, whisk yogurt, water, cornstarch, and salt. Place over medium heat and stir constantly until it gently boils.
  6. Cook Dumplings: Add shish barak pieces one by one into the yogurt. Simmer for about 30 minutes.
  7. Finish: Fry crushed garlic and cilantro in a small pan. Add to the yogurt sauce right before serving.
Dinner Serves 3
